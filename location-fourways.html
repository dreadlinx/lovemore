Fourways and the suburbs around it, including Lonehill, Dainfern, Craigavon and Broadacres, are a mix of secure estates, townhouse complexes and busy commercial areas. Plumbing in these settings often involves shared infrastructure and rules set by a body corporate or homeowners' association.

What to know if you're in Fourways

Before booking work in a complex or estate, check which pipes are yours and which are shared, and whether your managing agent needs to approve or be told about the work. We can inspect the problem and explain what we find in plain language, so you have what you need if a shared line turns out to be involved. Geysers in roof spaces are also worth checking regularly, since a slow leak up there can damage ceilings before it becomes obvious.

A common question in Fourways

Who is responsible for a plumbing fault in an estate or complex?

It usually depends on where the fault is. Pipes that serve only your home are typically your responsibility, while shared lines usually belong to the body corporate or homeowners' association, but check your scheme rules. We can inspect and give you a clear diagnosis to take to your managing agent.
