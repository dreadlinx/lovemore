Water Backup Tanks & Whole-Home Filtration

Johannesburg's unpredictable water cuts make emergency backup tanks an essential upgrade. We install automated multi-tank water storage with in-line booster pumps and medical-grade multi-stage filtration.

  • Clean sediment & carbon filtration for safe drinking water.
