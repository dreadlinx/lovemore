Acoustic & Thermal Tracing

Signs of a hidden leak

An unexplained jump in your water bill is the most common tell. Others include a damp patch on a wall or under flooring that won't dry out, the sound of running water when every tap is closed, or a water meter that keeps ticking over with everything in the house switched off. Our water-saving guide covers a simple meter test you can run yourself before calling us.

How we find it

We use listening equipment and, where needed, thermal imaging to narrow down a leak's location before opening anything up. This non-invasive approach means we can usually pinpoint a leak within a small area rather than guessing and cutting into multiple walls.

After detection

Once we've confirmed the location, we'll walk you through repair options — including whether the fix falls under our emergency service if it's actively causing damage, or can be scheduled as planned work.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Watch for an unexplained rise in your water bill, damp patches that don't dry out, or the sound of running water with all taps closed. A simple check: note your water meter reading, avoid using any water for an hour, then check it again — movement means a leak somewhere.

Will you need to break tiles or walls?

Not usually to find the leak — our detection equipment lets us narrow the location without demolition. Some repairs still require opening a small section of wall or floor, which we'll confirm and quote before starting.

How much does leak detection cost?

It depends on the property and suspected leak type. We'll give you a fixed price for the detection visit itself before we start, separate from any repair cost that follows.
