Common Questions

How quickly can you get to me in an emergency?

Emergencies are our priority and our emergency line is answered 24/7. Call us, tell us where you are, and we'll give you an honest arrival estimate before you commit to anything.

Do you charge for quotes?

Quotes for planned work such as installations and renovations are free. For emergency call-outs, we confirm any call-out charge with you by phone before a plumber is dispatched.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes. We're PIRB-registered and IOPSA members, our work is fully insured, and we use SABS-approved materials.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Orange Grove and cover Johannesburg and the surrounding Gauteng suburbs — see our full list on the Areas We Serve page, or ask us directly if your suburb isn't listed.
