About Us

A local plumbing business that grew up without losing its manners

Lovemore's Plumbing started as a one-person operation in Orange Grove. Over 10 years later, we're still run the same way — direct answers, fair pricing and a clean site when we leave.

Over 10 Years in Johannesburg

Serving Johannesburg for over 10 years

Lovemore's Plumbing was founded with a simple aim: reliable plumbing at a fair price, done by people who actually live in the community they work in. What began as a single plumber taking calls in Orange Grove has grown into a small team covering homes and businesses across Johannesburg.

As a family-owned business we rely on word of mouth and repeat customers, so we would rather do a job properly once than have to come back to it.

Three values that shape every job

Integrity

We quote honestly and explain what we find. If a repair is simpler (and cheaper) than expected, we tell you — we don't manufacture extra work.

Quality

We use SABS-approved materials and don't cut corners on fittings you can't see. A repair should outlast the invoice.

Reliability

We aim to arrive when we say we will, and to leave your space as clean as we found it.
