Our office and workshop are in Orange Grove, so central and northern Johannesburg is our home patch. It's a wide spread of property types: older freestanding homes in suburbs like Norwood, Highlands North and Sandringham, plus flats and townhouse complexes where pipes, geysers and drainage are shared between units.

What to know if you're in Johannesburg

Older houses often still rely on their original pipework, which is where slow leaks, corroded connections and recurring drain blockages tend to start. If you live in a complex, find out which plumbing is yours and which is shared before you call anyone; a good plumber will help you work that out and put the findings in writing for your managing agent. Water supply interruptions are also a fact of life in parts of Johannesburg, so it's worth knowing where your stopcock is and keeping it accessible.
