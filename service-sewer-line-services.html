Signs of a sewer line problem

Repeated blockages in the same drain, gurgling sounds from multiple fixtures at once, sewage smells in the garden, or a noticeably greener, soggier patch of lawn can all point to a damaged sewer line rather than a simple blockage — often caused by tree root intrusion in older pipe joints, common across Johannesburg's established suburbs.

Camera inspection first

Before recommending any excavation, we run a camera down the line to see exactly what's happening — root intrusion, a cracked joint, or a fully collapsed section — and exactly where. It also gives you a clear picture of the problem, which helps when you are comparing quotes or dealing with an insurer or body corporate.

Relining vs replacement

Where the pipe run allows it, no-dig pipe relining fixes the damaged section from the inside without digging up a garden or driveway. For collapsed sections or where relining isn't suitable, we excavate and replace the damaged length only, not the full run.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are signs of a collapsed sewer line?

Repeated blockages in the same spot, gurgling from multiple drains at once, sewage odours outdoors, or an unusually soggy, greener patch of lawn over the pipe run.

Do you need to dig up my garden?

Not always. Where the damage suits it, we use no-dig relining from the inside. Excavation is reserved for collapsed sections or where relining isn't viable, and we limit it to the affected length.

How much does sewer relining cost vs replacement?

It depends on the pipe diameter, length and the access available on site. A camera inspection lets us give you a firm, itemised price for both options so you can compare before deciding.
