Benoni & East Rand Teams

Benoni's suburbs, from Northmead and Rynfield to Lakefield and Farrarmere, are largely made up of family homes, where hot water and drains work hard every day. That everyday use is usually behind slow baths, running toilets and geysers that have reached the end of their life.

What to know if you're in Benoni

A full household puts more demand on the geyser and on every drain, so small warning signs are worth acting on early: a slow shower, a gurgling basin or a drip from the geyser's overflow pipe. If your family home is older, ask us about a leak check as well, since ageing pipework can waste water quietly for a long time before it shows.

A common question in Benoni

How often should a family home have its geyser checked?

There's no fixed rule, but a check every year or two is sensible, especially once a geyser is getting on in years. Discoloured water, rumbling noises or drips from the overflow pipe are all reasons to book sooner.
