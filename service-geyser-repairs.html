Signs your geyser needs attention

Geysers rarely fail without warning. Watch for discoloured or metallic-tasting water, a popping or rumbling sound from the tank, a geyser that trips the electrical circuit repeatedly, or water pooling near the drip tray. We cover each of these in more detail in our geyser warning signs guide.

Installation & repair

We work on both electric and solar geyser systems: element and thermostat replacement, vacuum breaker and pressure control valve servicing, drip tray and overflow pipe corrections, and full unit replacements when a tank has reached the end of its life. Solar conversions are increasingly popular given Johannesburg's electricity costs — we can quote both options side by side.

Compliance certificates

Geyser installations in South Africa need to meet SANS 10254 requirements, and many insurers ask for a valid Certificate of Compliance when you claim for geyser damage. As PIRB-registered plumbers, we can provide one with your installation — just ask when you book.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a geyser installation take?

A like-for-like replacement can often be done in a single visit. Solar conversions or hard-to-reach roof installations take longer, and we'll confirm a time estimate with your quote.

Electric or solar — which is better for Johannesburg?

Solar geysers cost more upfront but reduce electricity bills significantly over time and cope well with Johannesburg's sunny climate. Electric geysers are cheaper to install and simpler to repair. We can quote both so you can compare.

Do I need a compliance certificate after a geyser replacement?

Many insurers ask for one when you claim for geyser damage, so it's worth getting a Certificate of Compliance with every installation. Ask us about it when you book.
