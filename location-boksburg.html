Boksburg & Sunward Park Specialists

Boksburg's established suburbs, including Bardene, Parkrand, Sunward Park and Beyers Park, are known for freestanding family homes on decent-sized stands. Mature trees make these streets pleasant to live on, and they're also a common reason for older drain lines to give trouble.

What to know if you're in Boksburg

If a drain blocks again soon after it has been cleared, the cause is usually further along the line than the fitting you can see. A camera inspection then saves money, because it shows whether you're dealing with a simple build-up, roots or a damaged section before anyone starts digging. Exposed garden taps and pipes are also worth checking before winter, since Highveld frost can damage fittings that aren't protected.

A common question in Boksburg

My drain keeps blocking in the same place. Is a camera inspection worth it?

Usually, yes. Repeated blockages in one spot often point to a structural problem such as roots or a damaged joint, and clearing it again only buys time. A camera inspection shows the cause so you can decide on the right repair.
