What to know if you're in Midrand

New builds tend to need attention to snagging items such as slow drains, weak water pressure or fittings that need adjusting. If your home is still under a builder's warranty, check its terms before any repair work starts. A common question in Midrand

I'm in a new-build home. Can you help with plumbing snags?

Yes. Common snags include slow drains, weak water pressure and drips from fittings that weren't fully tightened. If the home is still under a builder's warranty, check the terms first so repairs don't affect your cover.
