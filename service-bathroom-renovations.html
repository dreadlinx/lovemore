We handle the full plumbing side of your bathroom renovation — from luxury freestanding baths and walk-in showers to custom tapware and complete pipe relocation.

Full Plumbing & Fixture Remodeling

What we cover

Bath, shower, toilet and basin installation, moving water and waste points to fit a new layout, luxury tapware fitting, and connecting new fixtures to existing supply and drainage lines. We can work alongside your builder and tiler, or coordinate the full renovation team on your behalf.

Moving fixtures, not just replacing them

A common renovation request is repositioning a toilet or basin to open up floor space. This means relocating waste and water points under the floor or through the wall — more involved than a straight swap, so we assess the existing pipe layout early and flag it in the quote rather than mid-renovation.

Timeline

The plumbing side of a standard bathroom renovation is usually a matter of days rather than weeks, split between a rough-in stage before tiling and a final-fit stage afterwards. The exact timing depends on how much of the layout is changing and whether a geyser upgrade is part of the project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle tiling too?

Our team focuses on the plumbing side — pipework, fixtures and tapware. We regularly work alongside tilers and builders and are happy to coordinate timing with your other trades.

Can you move my toilet or basin to a new position?

Yes. It requires relocating the waste and water points, which we assess and quote upfront once we've seen the existing layout, since it's more involved than a like-for-like replacement.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

The plumbing is usually a matter of days rather than weeks, split between a rough-in stage before tiling and a final-fit stage afterwards. We'll give you a realistic schedule with your quote.
